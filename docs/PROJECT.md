# Project: Myanmar Digital Menu & Order SaaS

## Goal
Build an affordable, mobile-first restaurant SaaS for Myanmar teahouses, cafes, and small restaurants. Customers scan table QR codes to browse menus and place orders. Restaurant owners manage menus, tables, orders, payments, and sales through a web dashboard.

## Target Users
- Restaurant owners
- Restaurant staff
- Dine-in customers
- SaaS platform administrators

## MVP Features
- Restaurant registration and authentication
- Multi-tenant restaurant management
- Burmese and English digital menus
- Menu categories, items, prices in MMK, and availability
- Table management and QR code generation
- Customer cart and order submission
- Staff order dashboard and status updates
- Cash and manually confirmed KBZPay, WavePay, and AYA Pay QR payments
- Basic order history and daily sales reports

## Business Model
Monthly SaaS subscriptions with configurable plans and future premium features.

## Product Principles
- Mobile-first and easy to use
- Affordable for small Myanmar businesses
- Fast on ordinary mobile devices and networks
- Secure isolation between restaurants
- Simple MVP before advanced features
- No automated payment verification without an approved provider integration

## Success Criteria
A restaurant can register, configure its menu, generate table QR codes, receive customer orders, manage fulfillment, and record payments without developer assistance.
